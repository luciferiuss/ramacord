DeepFilterNet3 noise suppression (WASM)
=====================================

Engine: DeepFilterNet3 by Hendrik Schröter et al.
  https://github.com/Rikorose/DeepFilterNet
  License: MIT OR Apache-2.0

Browser build and embedded model weights:
  denoise-voice-clarity (npm) by rajan471
  https://github.com/rajan471/denoise-voice-clarity
  License: MIT (see LICENSE)

The WASM file contains the DeepFilterNet3 weights; it is served from this
server and processed entirely in the participant's browser. No audio leaves
the device.
